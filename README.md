# Blumebyte Digital Solutions

Build a polished, modern, responsive company website for Blumebyte (blumebyte.com), a Ghana-based technology and digital solutions company. The brand should feel premium, practical, innovative, trustworthy, and business-focused rather than overly futuristic.

Core company positioning: Blumebyte makes technology easy and accessible for businesses and organizations, helping them improve operations, communication, productivity, and digital growth.

Primary services to feature:
1. Web Design & Custom Dashboards — modern responsive websites and intelligent dashboards built to streamline operations and improve decision-making.
2. Hosting & Domain Solutions — reliable domain registration and secure hosting services to keep businesses online and accessible.
3. Mobile App & Game Development — custom mobile apps and interactive game experiences designed to engage users and support digital growth.
4. Virtual Assistance — professional remote support for administrative tasks, communication management, and digital workflow coordination.
5. SmartSuite Solutions — integrated productivity solutions that help teams manage projects, documents, communication, and internal processes efficiently.
6. PrintTech Supplies (Toners & Cartridges) — quality printer toners and cartridges for consistent office and business printing.
7. E-commerce — complete online store setup and management solutions that help businesses sell products and services online.

Also include Blumebyte HR as a featured product/solution: a lightweight HR management platform for small and growing organizations, supporting employee records, leave, attendance, payroll/people workflows, and internal HR processes. Position it as one of Blumebyte's own digital products, with a prominent CTA to learn more or request a demo.

Website structure/pages:
- Home
- About
- Services
- Blumebyte HR / Products
- Blog / Insights
- Portfolio / Projects
- Contact

Homepage sections:
- Strong hero headline focused on helping businesses work smarter with technology
- Supporting subheading and CTAs: “Get Started” and “Explore Our Services”
- Services overview grid
- Featured Blumebyte HR product section
- Why Choose Blumebyte section
- Simple process section (Discover, Design, Build, Support)
- Portfolio/project showcase placeholders
- Latest Insights/blog teaser section
- Client testimonial placeholders
- Strong final CTA and full footer

Use concise, professional copy throughout. Avoid buzzword overload. Mention Ghana where useful but keep the brand suitable for African and international clients.

Visual direction:
- Clean modern SaaS/technology aesthetic
- White or very light base with strong dark text and a refined blue/purple accent palette
- Generous whitespace, rounded cards, subtle shadows, strong typography, tasteful gradients
- High-quality icons and abstract tech visuals where appropriate
- Fully responsive mobile/tablet/desktop
- Professional navigation with sticky header
- Smooth section transitions and tasteful hover interactions
- Accessible contrast and semantic structure

Functional requirements:
- Contact form with fields for name, email, phone, company, service of interest, and message
- Blog listing page with cards, categories, tags, date, author, and search/filter UI
- Seed the blog with several tech/business article placeholders, including topics like AI coding tools, digital transformation, AI infrastructure, and business technology trends
- Portfolio/project cards with category tags and results-oriented summaries
- Service detail sections/cards with clear calls to action
- SEO-friendly page titles, descriptions, headings, and social meta defaults
- Add a WhatsApp-style contact CTA/button but keep the number configurable in one obvious place
- Footer with quick links, services, contact info placeholders, and social links

Use TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, and shadcn/ui. Create reusable components and a polished production-quality frontend. No authentication or database is needed for this first version. Make the site easy to extend later with a CMS or Supabase if needed.
